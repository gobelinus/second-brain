# LLM Wiki Pattern

Cross-links: [[second-brain]] · [[topic-context]]

## Learnings
- Karpathy's "LLM Wiki" reframes a personal knowledge base as **immutable timestamped capture at the bottom, synthesized views derived on top** — explicitly an alternative to re-retrieving raw documents (RAG). It draws on Vannevar Bush's 1945 Memex but solves the maintenance burden by having the LLM handle cross-references and consistency. ([2026-06-27](../../journal/2026-06-27.md#second-brain-architecture))
