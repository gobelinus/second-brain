## Research Notes
### Why RAG over Fine-tuning?
RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation) is the right pattern for a personal knowledge base:
- Fine-tuning is expensive, slow, and requires retraining on every new entry
- RAG gives the LLM your data as live context — no retraining needed
- New daily summaries are immediately queryable after ingestion
- Source data stays in plain markdown — always human readable and inspectable

### Dual Store Design
Two complementary stores serve different query types:
- SQLite handles structured queries: counts, trends, date ranges, aggregations
- ChromaDB handles semantic queries: meaning-based search, similarity, summaries
- They share `date` as a logical key — query router decides which store leads

### Local-Only Architecture Benefits
- Zero data privacy risk — nothing leaves the machine
- No API costs — runs entirely on local compute
- Works offline — no internet dependency after model pull
- Full control over data retention and deletion
